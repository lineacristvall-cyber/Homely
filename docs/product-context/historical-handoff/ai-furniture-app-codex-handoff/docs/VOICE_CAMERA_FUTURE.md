# Future Voice + Camera Concept

This is preserved as product context, not V0 scope.

## Voice

Possible uses:

- rapid request creation
- conversational refinement
- rejection feedback
- hands-free sourcing while moving through a space

Example:

> I need six dining chairs for this walnut table. Keep them under $350 each. Warm Scandinavian, a little vintage. No bouclé or black metal.

The system converts that into structured constraints and shows the parsed version.

## Camera

Possible uses:

- show an existing furniture piece
- show room proportions
- show material/color
- point to the area needing an item

## Combined live experience

Longer term, the user could have a voice conversation while pointing the camera at a room or piece of furniture.

The AI could combine:

- live visual context
- spoken constraints
- project context
- search

## V0 preparation

V0 only needs:

- reference-image upload
- image association with request/existing item
- modality-neutral domain model

Do not build live camera or voice infrastructure yet.
